      Five stamps a day

      Five countries, five challenges, one new passport page every day. Each challenge is harder than the last and worth more: 100, 150, 200, 250, 300. Up to 1,000 points a day.

      __explorer__Climb the ranks. Every point is XP. Start as a Tourist, finish as a Legend.
      __outline__Eight kinds of challenge. Outlines, flags, capitals, borders, odd one out, bigger or smaller, and dropping a pin on the globe.
      __check__How scoring works. Full points first time, 40% on a second try. Pins pay more the closer you land, and small countries are judged more strictly.
      __hint__Stuck? A hint costs a quarter of that challenge’s points.
      __book__Fill your passport. Every country you get right is stamped in for good. Can you collect all 195?
      __expert__Too easy? Expert mode in Settings makes you type the answers.
