      Five stamps a day

      Every day your passport has five challenges, picked from eight kinds. They get harder as you go, and later stamps are worth more: 100, 150, 200, 250, 300. That’s up to 1,000 a day.

      __explorer__Traveller rank: every point you score is XP. Climb from Tourist to Legend.
      __outline__Eight kinds: Outline, Flag, Capital, Whose capital?, Borders, Odd one out, Bigger, and Pin it.
      __check__Scoring: full points first time, 40% on a second try. Pin it pays by distance, scaled to the country’s size. Bigger pays for 3+ right out of 5. Score 60% of a stamp’s value to earn it.
      __hint__Stuck? A hint costs a quarter of that stamp’s points.
      __book__Every country you get right is stamped into your passport book. Can you fill all 195?
      __expert__Too easy? Turn on Expert mode in Settings to type your answers.
