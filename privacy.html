Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Passport is a free daily geography game. You can play without giving us any personal details. If you choose to save your passport with an email address, this page explains what we keep and why.

The short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling data. Without an account we only store an anonymous score. With an account we store your email and your passport, and you can delete both at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Passport (playpassport.club) is an independent game run from the United Kingdom. It is the data controller for the information described here. For anything about your data, contact hello@playpassport.club.

What we store

WhatWhy, and our lawful basis
Your game progress on your deviceYour stamps, streak, rank and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage so the game remembers you. It stays on your device and we can’t see it. It’s needed for the game to work.
Your daily scoreWhen you finish a day, we store the puzzle number, your score and a random ID created by your browser. It isn’t linked to your name or email. We use it to show how you compare with other players. Legitimate interests: running the game.
League detailsIf you start or join a league, we store the league name and the display name you type, linked to your random ID, so your league can see each other’s scores. Legitimate interests: providing a feature you asked for.
Your email address (only if you save your passport)Used to sign you in with a link or code. We never use it for marketing and never share it. Contract: providing the account you asked for.
Your saved passport (only with an account)A backup of your stamps, rank and streak, so you can carry on from another device. Contract: providing the account you asked for.
Technical logsOur hosting and database providers keep short-lived logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure. Legitimate interests: security.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we never sell or rent your data.

Cookies and local storage

Passport uses your browser’s local storage only to save your game, and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. This is strictly necessary for the features you use, so there’s no cookie banner. Clearing your browser data deletes this progress unless you’ve saved your passport to an account.

Who helps us run Passport

These providers process data on our behalf, under their own data protection terms:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service. It stores daily scores, leagues, accounts and saved passports.
  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft): hosts the website.
  • Resend: sends sign-in emails.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the typefaces and the sign-in code library. They receive your IP address when your browser loads these files.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards approved under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Daily scores and league entries: kept while the game runs, so league tables and comparisons stay accurate. They don’t identify you.
  • Your account and saved passport: kept until you delete them. If an account isn’t used for 2 years, we may delete it.
  • Provider logs: kept for the periods set by those providers, usually days or weeks.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it.

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your email and saved passport straight away.
  • Anything else: email hello@playpassport.club. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Children

Anyone can play without an account. Please only save your passport with an email address if you’re 13 or older, or have a parent’s or guardian’s permission.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes affecting account holders, we’ll tell you in the game first.
